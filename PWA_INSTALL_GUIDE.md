# How to Install Daily Tasks (PWA) on iPhone & Android

This guide explains how to install the **Daily Tasks** app on your phone so it behaves like a native app (runs outside browser tabs, has its own app icon, and receives push notifications).

---

## 📱 Installation on Android (Chrome / Firefox)

Android makes PWA installation very easy.

1. **Open Google Chrome** (or Firefox) on your Android device.
2. Navigate to your app's hosted URL (e.g. your Vercel URL: `https://[your-app-subdomain].vercel.app`).
3. You will likely see an **"Add Daily Tasks to Home screen"** banner pop up at the bottom. Tap it!
4. **If you don't see the banner:**
   - Tap the **three vertical dots** (menu button) in the top-right corner of Chrome.
   - Select **"Install app"** (or **"Add to Home screen"**).
5. A confirmation dialog will appear. Tap **Install**.
6. The app is now installed and will appear on your Home Screen and App Drawer with its own icon.

---

## 🍎 Installation on iPhone / iOS (Safari)

Apple requires you to add the app manually from Safari to enable native behaviors and Web Push notifications.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Web Push notifications on iOS** require iOS 16.4 or later, and the app **must** be installed to your Home Screen. It will not receive background notifications in regular browser tabs.

1. **Open Safari** on your iPhone. (Do *not* use Chrome or third-party browsers for this step, as they cannot register Home Screen apps).
2. Navigate to your app's hosted URL.
3. Tap the **Share button** (the square icon with an arrow pointing up) at the bottom center of the screen.
4. Scroll down the share sheet menu and tap **"Add to Home Screen"**.
5. Choose a name (e.g. "Daily Tasks") and tap **Add** in the top-right corner.
6. The app will immediately appear on your iPhone Home Screen.

---

## 🔔 Enabling Push Notifications After Installation

Once the app is installed on your Home Screen, open it and follow these steps to activate phone notifications:

1. Launch the **Daily Tasks** app from your Home Screen.
2. Log in to your account.
3. Go to the **Settings** menu.
4. Locate the **Notifications** section.
5. If you see a **"Tap to allow browser notifications"** link, tap it.
6. When your phone asks: *"Daily Tasks Would Like to Send You Notifications"*, tap **Allow**.
7. Toggle the **Notifications Enabled** switch **ON**.

You are now fully set up to receive instant push alerts whenever your partner sends a sealed message or updates a task!
